How CampusConnect Works

From event creation to real-time attendee management in 3 simple steps

1. Colleges Publish

Institutions create verified accounts and publish hackathons, symposiums, robotics bootcamps, and cultural fests with complete rules and venue schedules.

2. Students Discover

Students from any college in India discover events based on city, state, or category using our fast, multi-filtered discovery engine.

3. Instant Registration & Passes

One-click registration generates a unique digital pass ID with verification codes, while organizers monitor attendance live.
